## 4. Criminal offences (Referral to court, not fines)

Federal Decree-Law 14/2024 (VERIFIED, uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2598):
- **Art. 38**: deliberately not stopping after an accident with injuries; owner withholding information; fleeing police; deliberate collision with police vehicles. Up to 2 years' prison and fine AED 50,000-100,000 (numerals; the English text prints "one million" in words, an apparent typo), or either.
- **Art. 39**: crossing the road outside designated places: if it causes an accident, prison and AED 5,000-10,000; on roads with speed limit >= 80 km/h, at least 3 months and at least AED 10,000.
- **Art. 40**: causing death by fault: prison and at least AED 50,000; at least 1 year and AED 100,000 if due to red light, intoxication, suspended licence, or driving in a flooded valley.
- Art. 35 (DUI), Art. 36 (suspended licence), Art. 37 (no licence), Art. 41 (refusing personal data), Art. 32 (vehicle seizure grounds: unfit vehicle, unlicensed driver, substantial modification, accident involving a crime).
These must be reported as "Referral to court / prosecution", with no AED fine from the schedule.

## 6. Gaps and cautions
1. No Cabinet resolution replacing MR 178/2017 found; re-check uaelegislation.gov.ae (sector "Security and Safety") before the demo, since Art. 49(1) required executive regulations by about late Sept 2025.
2. The MR178 PDF copy read is the base table. Amendments (2019 emergency-vehicle, 2023 valley rows, possible other changes) are only SECONDARY. An up-to-date consolidated table (MOI Arabic page linked from u.ae) was not opened.
3. Black-point/impound column alignment in the PDF text extraction is slightly ambiguous for a few rows (seatbelt, item 19, item 48). Values above follow the extraction; spot-check against the PDF visually before printing in a report.
4. No official row for fatigue/drowsy driving, unsafe lane change by name, or roundabout priority.
5. Dubai AED 520 and Dubai impound release fees are not officially verified.
