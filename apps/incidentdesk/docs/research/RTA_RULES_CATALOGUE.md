# RTA / Dubai / UAE road rules catalogue for deciding fault (research, 2026-10-18)

Research only. Nothing here changes code, prompts, schemas or truth. It **extends** `UAE_ROAD_STRUCTURE_AND_PRIORITY.md` (rows R1-R19, S1-S12, F1-F8, fields P1-P24) and `UAE_TRAFFIC_FINES_VERIFIED.md`; it does not repeat their reasoning. Where a rule is already there, the row here points to it (`see R8`) and adds what was missing: the exact article text, the typed facts our schema has (or lacks), and whether the engine covers it.

Trigger: the 2026-10-18 benchmark run hit four rule gaps (left turn across oncoming traffic, roundabout exits, heavy vehicles / buses / motorcycles, shared fault).

**Status labels** (same as the two earlier notes):
- **VERIFIED** = primary text read in this session on an official site (uaelegislation.gov.ae, u.ae, mediaoffice.ae, RTA's drivedubai.ae). For the RTA handbooks this means "verified as RTA guidance", not statute.
- **SECONDARY** = news, law firm, blog, third-party copy of an official document, or an official document seen only as a search excerpt.
- **UNVERIFIED** = looked for and not confirmed. **Absent** = looked for in a full primary text and not there.

**Translation.** MR 130/1997 is official only in Arabic. Every English rendering of it below is my own plain translation, with a short Arabic key phrase so a reader can check it. It is not a certified translation (see open question Q9 in the earlier note).

**Columns.** *Fields* uses names from `src/incident_report/facts.py` `FIELD_VALUES` (plus `posted_speed_limit_kmh`). A field the rule needs that is **not** in `FIELD_VALUES` is marked `GAP:` and named after the earlier note's P-field where one exists. *Engine* is what `src/incident_report/rules/engine.py` + `core.py` do today (read 2026-10-18 at `71342b6`): **covered** = a rule function can return a Finding; **partial** = covered only for some party types or geometries; **no** = the combination ends in "Uncovered typed-field combination", "not covered", "unresolved" or Cannot Determine.

**One engine fact that matters for every row:** `evaluate_situation` sends vehicle pairs to the vehicle rules only when **both** party types are `car`, `taxi` or `emergency_or_official_marked`. Any pair with `truck_or_heavy`, `bus`, `motorcycle`, `bicycle_or_personal_transport` or `other` returns "party_type pairing is not covered by these core rules", even for a red light or a rear-end. The pedestrian rule likewise runs only against a `car` or `taxi`.

Method: Agent Reach. Jina Reader for the official texts (MR 130/1997 Arabic full text, Decree-Law 14/2024 English, MR 178/2017 table and Arabic definitions, both RTA handbooks, Dubai Media Office); Exa search (rate-limited after about ten calls) for discovery and secondary sources; Reddit only as Exa excerpts, for practice anecdotes. Access date for every source: 2026-10-18.

**Answer for gap 1.** The rule text is MR 130/1997 Art. 59: a driver turning into a side road must let "vehicles coming from the opposite direction ... pass first" (59(5)), after checking others' positions and speeds (59(2)) and signalling in good time (59(3)). Decree-Law 14/2024 Art. 6 does not deal with oncoming turns at all. At a Stop or Give Way approach, the RTA handbook (p.100) also tells the turner to give way to traffic "coming towards you". No MR 178/2017 row names this breach, so the officer picks the item.

**Owner case, read neutrally against the text.** Facts as given: car A leaves the roundabout, then turns left across the inbound lanes into a side road; car B goes straight on the inbound lane towards the roundabout; they collide.
- **If the side-road junction is a separate junction outside the roundabout** (the RTA handbook's description puts the roundabout at the signed entrances, central island and marked lanes), then A is "turning left to enter a side road" and Art. 59(5) tells A to let "vehicles coming from the opposite direction ... pass first". B is oncoming to A. On this reading the text puts the duty to yield on A. B still owes Art. 42 (approach speed), Art. 38 (speed that lets him stop within sight), and Art. 45 (do not insist on priority), but those are care duties, not a duty to wait.
- **If the junction counts as part of the roundabout**, Art. 44(1) / Decree-Law Art. 6 give priority to "those coming from the left", and the handbook makes *entering* drivers give way to vehicles "already in it". Whether A (leaving) is still "in" the roundabout, and whether A's path counts as "from the left" of B, is not answered by any text found. Arguing that B must wait needs this reading plus an answer to both of those points.
- **Signs decide first.** If B's approach has a give-way line, its legal effect is at that line (Art. 27; R3). If the conflict point is before the line, the line does not obviously reach it (my inference, not a source). If the side road or the turning area has its own sign, that sign wins (Art. 44 applies only when there is no officer, sign or signal).
- **Practice anecdote (not evidence):** r/UAE, 9 Jan 2026 (https://www.reddit.com/r/UAE/comments/1q85glh/): a driver leaving a three-lane roundabout from the inner lane was hit from behind by a pickup in the middle lane and was given the fine and "liable party paper". Different geometry, but it shows police can blame the exiting driver who crosses lanes. SECONDARY / anecdote.
- Net: the published text supports "A yields" only under the separate-junction reading; it supports "B waits" only if the owner or an RTA/Dubai Police source rules that the junction is part of the roundabout. See open question Q1.

## 3. Open questions for the owner

1. **Roundabout boundary (owner case).** No UAE text defines where a roundabout ends. Do we treat a side-road junction on an approach arm, outside the circulating markings, as a separate junction (then MR 130 Art. 59(5) puts the yield duty on the left-turning exiting car), or as part of the roundabout (then Art. 44(1) "from the left" is argued, and it is unclear whether an exiting car is still "in" it)? Until there is a ruling or an RTA / Dubai Police source, the safe output is Cannot Determine with this reason. Can the professor ask Dubai Police how they code this case?
2. **Oncoming left-turn item.** Art. 59(5) is clear, but no MR 178/2017 row names "not letting oncoming traffic pass first". Do we report the finding under Art. 59 with "officer selects the schedule item" (as for lane changes today), or map it to Item 99b or Item 84? Recommended: officer selects (no invented mapping).
3. **Does Art. 16-17 (الدراجات) cover motorcycles?** If yes, "weaving" and "keep right, single file" become motorcycle duties. Needs a legal reading or an RTA answer.
4. **Party-type gate.** Should the existing car rules (red light, rear-end, lane change, entry, give-way) also run for trucks, buses and motorcycles? The statutes (Art. 25, 43, 50, 58-60) apply to every "vehicle"; only Art. 67-70 add heavy-vehicle duties. This is scope and engine design, so it is the owner's call.
5. **Art. 45 (do not insist on priority).** Encode it? Video rarely proves the priority holder could have stopped. Options: never a Finding; or a Contributing Factor only when an observable cue (for example, a clear view of a stopped or turning vehicle well before contact) is defined and approved. No threshold may be invented (ADR 0006).
6. **Fatigue citation.** MR 130/1997 Art. 30 bans driving while exhausted. Should `drowsiness.py` cite it instead of "Project observation rule; not a legal duty"? There is still no fine row.
7. **Guidance-only rules.** Roundabout exit lane discipline, the 1 m clearance for riders, truck blind spots and left-turn give-way at Stop signs exist only in the RTA handbooks. May the engine cite a handbook page (flagged "RTA guidance"), or only statutes? (Same as Q12 in the earlier note, now with more rows depending on it.)
8. **Delivery-bike lane ban amounts.** AED 500 / 700 come from an official announcement, not from MR 178/2017. Show as provisional, or omit until the instrument is found?
9. **Shared fault display.** The engine shows both parties as Cannot Determine with both breaches listed (ruling 2026-10-15). Secondary sources say the traffic expert sets fault percentages. Keep the current display? (ADR 0004 bars percentages either way.)
10. **Executive Regulations of Decree-Law 14/2024.** Art. 5(2) leaves heavy-vehicle, transport and personal-transport duties to them. Still not found. If they appear, rows C19-C22 and C28 may change.
