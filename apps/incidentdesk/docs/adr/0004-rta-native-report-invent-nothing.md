# The report is RTA / Dubai Police-native: invent no categories, scales or layouts

Officers must recognise the report as the kind of document they already work with. This applies to everything:
- fault statuses (the Dubai Police pink, green and white scheme)
- severity categories
- violation names and fines (current UAE traffic law)
- section layout and wording

These follow the real RTA and Dubai Police practice. They are never categories we designed ourselves.

Where the official form or scale is not yet known, mark the field as provisional and ask the professor for a real (redacted) Dubai Police / RTA accident report. Do not design a new scheme to fill the gap.

This rules out invented scales, such as fault percentages or a home-made severity rubric, even where they would be easier to score.

## Amendment 2026-10-16 (owner, RPT-56)

White is used only in its RTA meaning: the form issued when neither party is accused, or the other party is unknown (`docs/research/UAE_ROAD_STRUCTURE_AND_PRIORITY.md`, F1), and only when a cited rule and the facts support it. Cannot Determine is a separate state that belongs to this report, not to the police scheme. It is shown as "Not determined by this report" with a neutral grey chip and is never presented as a police form colour. Where this ADR earlier said "pink, green and white" for fault statuses, read it as pink, green and white in the RTA sense, plus grey for Cannot Determine.
